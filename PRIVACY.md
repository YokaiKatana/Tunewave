# Privacy policy

Tunewave is an open-source desktop player for YouTube Music. It is not
affiliated with Google, YouTube or Spotify.

## What Tunewave does with your data

- **No Tunewave servers.** There is no Tunewave backend and no account system.
  Tunewave never sends your data to the developer or to any third party of the
  developer's.
- **No telemetry, tracking or ads.** Tunewave does not collect analytics or
  usage data.
- **Your login stays on your computer.** When you sign in, Tunewave stores your
  YouTube / Google login on your own computer only, so you remain signed in
  between launches — the same way a web browser does. You can remove it at any
  time with *Account → Sign out & clear data*.
- **Your password is never seen by Tunewave.** You enter it only on Google's own
  sign-in page, in your own web browser. Tunewave copies only the resulting
  YouTube / Google login into the app.

## Information sent to other networked systems

Tunewave transfers information to another networked system only when you ask it
to:

- **Google / YouTube (music.youtube.com):** required to sign in and to play
  music. Your use of YouTube Music is covered by
  [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **GitHub (github.com):** Tunewave checks the public releases page to see
  whether a newer version is available. No personal data is sent.

Tunewave does not transfer information to any other networked system.

## Uninstalling

Tunewave is distributed as a portable app: delete the Tunewave folder to remove
it. To also remove your stored login and settings, use *Account → Sign out &
clear data* first, or delete the `Tunewave` folder under
`%APPDATA%` (Windows), `~/Library/Application Support/Tunewave` (macOS) or
`~/.config/Tunewave` (Linux).
