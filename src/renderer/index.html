Tunewave
Updates are checked automatically.

If you're already signed in to Google in your default browser (Firefox, Chrome, Edge, Brave), Tunewave reuses that login — often with no window at all. If you're not, it opens your browser to Google's sign-in page, waits for you to finish, then brings the login in. Tunewave only ever copies your YouTube/Google login and never sees your password. Your login is stored only on this computer.

Your account stays yours

  • Tunewave never sees your password. You type it only on Google's own sign-in page, in your own browser.
  • Your login is saved only on this computer, so you stay signed in between launches — the same way a web browser remembers you. Remove it any time with Sign out & clear data.
  • Nothing is sent to any Tunewave server — there isn't one, and there's no account system. The only place your login goes is Google / YouTube, which is required to play your music.
  • No tracking, ads or analytics. Tunewave is an open-source player; the full code is on GitHub.
